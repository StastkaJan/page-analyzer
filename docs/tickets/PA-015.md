# PA-015: Deliver the complete accessible actionable report

Stage: [S5 - Actionable reports and recommendations](../roadmap.md#s5-actionable-reports-and-recommendations)
Status: Not started
Depends on: [PA-013](PA-013.md), [PA-014](PA-014.md)

## Expected result

Present category findings and guidance in a report a reader can use to plan a fix.

## Scope

- Render evidence, targets, source scores, priority/rationale, effort, fixes, documentation, and verification steps.
- Keep missing scores, failures, skips, not-applicable checks, and manual review visible and distinct.
- Verify accessible navigation and safe content rendering; prepare representative reports for feedback.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reader can select a priority issue, inspect why it matters, and identify the next verification step.
- [ ] Successful, partial, and failed reports preserve evidence and limitations without disguising failures.
- [ ] Keyboard navigation, focus, readable status/error text, and hostile-content handling work throughout.

## Verification

- Compare rendered reports to source fixtures across all outcomes and complete keyboard/rendering checks.
- Record S5 samples and any available feedback; outreach needs an explicitly authorized task and cannot be claimed as completed otherwise.

## Out of scope

Contacting users automatically, AI explanations, and optional redesign unrelated to acceptance.

## Completion evidence

Not implemented. When completing this ticket, record changed files/PR, exact checks and outcomes, demonstration evidence, and remaining limitations here.
