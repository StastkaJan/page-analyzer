# PA-036: Define numeric admission and retention policies

Stage: [S7 - Operating limits, retention, and diagnostics](../roadmap.md#s7-operating-limits-retention-and-diagnostics)
Status: Not started
Depends on: [PA-018](PA-018.md), [PA-034](PA-034.md)

## Expected result

Provide concrete operating defaults and deletion semantics before enforcing quotas or cleanup.

## Scope

- Choose finite request rates/windows, principal/global queued-work caps, worker concurrency, report/artifact/log retention durations, cleanup cadence, and maximum deletion completion delay.
- Define admission responses and retry guidance, expiry visibility, authorized deletion, active-job/late-result treatment, orphan cleanup, and how references/comparisons lose access.
- State any backup/restore implications if backups are configured, and map every policy to an enforcement owner and setting. Reconcile with PA-033/PA-034 budgets.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All required numeric values include units/scope/rationale and behavior at the boundary; no vague 'reasonable' limit substitutes for a value.
- [ ] Deletion specifies when access stops and when data is physically removed, including retries after storage failure and prevention of result resurrection.
- [ ] Policies identify authoritative settings and are implementable by PA-019/PA-020 without choosing conflicting defaults.

## Verification

- Walk quota races, backlog, expiry, deletion during a scan, late delivery, and cleanup failure with explicit timeline examples.
- Check consistency with retained-report behavior and delivery/resource budgets; record limits as initial policy rather than measured production capacity.

## Out of scope

Implementing admission/cleanup, legal compliance claims, billing tiers, and provisioning backup infrastructure.
