# PA-020: Implement retention and deletion without result resurrection

Stage: [S7 - Operating limits, retention, and diagnostics](../roadmap.md#s7-operating-limits-retention-and-diagnostics)
Status: Not started
Depends on: [PA-036](PA-036.md)

## Expected result

Remove expired or owner-deleted reports and artifacts consistently despite active work.

## Scope

- Implement PA-036's retention durations, visibility/removal deadlines, cleanup cadence, and authorized deletion behavior for reports, artifacts, and comparisons.
- Coordinate active/retried jobs and late results so deletion cannot recreate captured data.
- Clean orphaned artifacts and retry cleanup safely after storage failures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deleted/expired records and artifact downloads become inaccessible according to policy.
- [ ] Late attempts/results cannot recreate deleted reports or orphan newly written artifacts indefinitely.
- [ ] Cleanup is repeatable and recoverable; unrelated users cannot delete reports and comparison access updates consistently.

## Verification

- Delete during scanning, replay late results, expire artifacts, and inject partial cleanup failure.
- Verify storage removal, denied downloads/comparisons, orphan cleanup, and documented retention behavior.

## Out of scope

New cancellation UI, legal retention claims, and preserving deleted page content for diagnostics.
